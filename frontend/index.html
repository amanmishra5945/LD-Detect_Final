About the Screening System & Ethical Framework

Understanding the engineering methodology, age-adapted difficulty, and non-clinical guidelines.

System Purpose & Ethical Safeguards

This software is developed as an intelligent educational screening aid designed to detect early observable indicators of Dyslexia and Dysgraphia in children aged 5 to 12. It provides structured, explainable indicators for educators and parents. It is NOT a clinical diagnostic system.

⚖️
Core Ethical Rule: The system never declares "Dyslexia detected" or "Dysgraphia confirmed." It returns "Screening result" and "Observed indicators."

Age-Adaptive Architecture (Ages 5–12)

The system organizes assessments into 4 distinct difficulty tiers that adapt automatically based on the child's recorded age:

  • Beginner (Ages 5–6): Single letter confusion pairs (b/d, p/q), 3-letter CVC sight words, 5-word sentences, and simple uppercase/lowercase handwriting.
  • Elementary (Ages 7–8): Common sight vocabulary, rhyming and compound sentences, basic word copying.
  • Intermediate (Ages 9–10): Multi-syllable vocabulary, multi-clause oral reading, sound segmentation, spatial paragraph handwriting.
  • Advanced (Ages 11–12): Complex vocabulary, paragraph comprehension, morphology, cursive/print consistency.

AI & Computer Vision Methodology

  • Speech Recognition & Alignment: Browser Web Speech API captures real-time spoken audio without storing raw voice recordings. Dynamic programming word-level alignment calculates substitutions, omissions, insertions, repetitions, and hesitation pauses.
  • Computer Vision Handwriting Analysis: OpenCV processes canvas ink density, contour count, and scale/rotation invariant Hu moments to measure character structure and detect letter-form mismatches (e.g. b/d loop reversal).
  • Kinematic Stroke Analysis: High-frequency pointer events capture velocity profiles, pen lifts, and stroke smoothness.
